Terms

Terms & Conditions

The framework behind every print job we deliver — from business cards and flyers to large-format banners, packaging and bulk printing. Clear, simple and designed to protect both sides of the agreement.

Last updated

January 2025

Every print job is produced according to the specifications agreed in the written quotation — including size, paper stock, finishing, quantity and turnaround. Print-ready files must be supplied in the correct format (PDF, AI, PSD or as specified), at the correct resolution (300 DPI minimum for standard print), with bleed and crop marks where required. Files that do not meet print-ready standards may require prepress work, which is billed separately.

A non-refundable deposit of 50% of the total print cost is required before production begins. The remaining balance is due upon completion and before delivery or pickup of the printed materials. Large-format, bulk or packaging orders may require full payment upfront. Corporate clients may request 30-day invoicing, subject to approval.

A digital proof is provided before printing. Written approval (email or WhatsApp confirmation) is required before production begins. Once a proof is approved, the client accepts full responsibility for any errors in text, spelling, layout, images or colours. Africana Tech & Branding Ltd is not liable for mistakes that were present in the approved proof. Physical proofs are available on request at an additional cost.

Turnaround times are quoted per job and begin from the date the deposit is received and the proof is approved. Standard jobs typically run 2–5 working days; bulk, packaging or large-format jobs may take longer. Delivery is available within Dar es Salaam and by courier nationwide at an additional cost. Risk of loss or damage passes to the client upon collection or handover to the courier.

The client confirms that all files, images, logos and content supplied for printing are either owned by them or properly licensed, and do not infringe on any third-party copyright, trademark or intellectual property rights. The client accepts full responsibility for any legal claims arising from supplied material. We reserve the right to refuse printing of content that is unlawful, offensive or infringing.

Screen colours displayed on a monitor may differ from the final printed result due to differences between RGB (screen) and CMYK (print) colour spaces, paper stock, ink and finishing. Minor variations in colour, tone and finish are normal and not considered a defect. If an exact colour match is critical, the client should request a physical proof or provide a Pantone reference before production.

Any defect in printing, cutting, binding or finishing caused by us will be corrected at our cost through a reprint or a partial refund. Claims must be reported in writing within 7 days of receiving the printed materials, along with photos and the affected samples. We are not responsible for defects arising from client-supplied files, incorrect briefs, or proofs approved by the client.

The 50% deposit is non-refundable once production has started. If the client cancels after the proof is approved, they will be billed for work completed, materials already purchased and any set-up costs incurred up to the cancellation date. Once printing has begun, orders cannot be cancelled or modified.

All files, designs and information shared with us for printing are treated as strictly confidential. We do not share, sell or disclose client files to any third party without explicit written consent, except where required by law. Client files are stored securely and may be deleted after 12 months unless the client requests otherwise.

By commissioning any print job with Africana Tech & Branding Ltd, the client agrees to the terms above.
