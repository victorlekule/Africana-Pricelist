      Terms & Conditions

      The framework behind every creative project we deliver — from logos and brand systems to social content, video and campaign work. Clear, simple and designed to protect both sides of the agreement.

      Last updated

      January 2025

      Every creative project starts with a written brief defining deliverables, formats, number of concepts and revisions. Any work not listed in the brief — additional concepts, new formats, extra reels or extended campaigns — is treated as out of scope and will require a separate quotation before it begins.

      A non-refundable deposit of 40% of the total project value is required before any creative work begins. The remaining 60% is due upon project completion and before final files, source files or published assets are handed over. Monthly retainers (social media, video, advertising) are invoiced at the start of each cycle. Corporate clients may request 30-day invoicing, subject to approval.

      Each deliverable includes two rounds of revisions at no extra cost. Further revisions are billed at a standard hourly rate. Feedback must be consolidated per round, and once a concept is approved in writing, changes thereafter are treated as a new request. Video and motion projects include one round of edit revisions unless stated otherwise in the brief.

      Creative timelines are quoted per project and begin from the date the deposit is received and all required content (text, images, brand assets, references) is supplied by the client. Delays caused by late feedback, missing assets or scope changes will extend the delivery date accordingly. Final files are delivered in agreed formats (PDF, PNG, JPG, MP4, AI, PSD, etc.).

      The client agrees to provide all required content — text, images, logos, brand guidelines, video footage and access credentials — within the agreed timeframe. The client confirms that all materials shared do not infringe on third-party copyrights or trademarks. The client is also responsible for timely approvals so the project stays on schedule.

      Upon full payment, ownership of the final approved deliverables transfers to the client, including usage rights for print, digital and broadcast as agreed in the brief. Africana Tech & Branding Ltd retains the right to display completed work in its portfolio, website and social media unless a written confidentiality agreement states otherwise. Rejected concepts, drafts and source files remain our property unless agreed otherwise.

      The client is responsible for verifying the accuracy of all information included in creative work — product details, prices, offers, claims and disclaimers. Advertising media spend (Meta, Google, TikTok and others) is billed separately and paid directly to the advertising platform. Africana Tech & Branding Ltd is not responsible for ad account suspensions, policy rejections or third-party platform decisions.

      The 40% deposit is non-refundable once creative work has begun. If the client cancels after work has started, they will be billed for work completed up to the cancellation date. Monthly retainers may be paused or cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Projects on hold for more than 60 days without communication are considered cancelled.

      All client information, business strategies, brand assets and internal data shared with us during a project are treated as strictly confidential. We do not share, sell or disclose client information to any third party without explicit written consent, except where required by law.

      By commissioning any creative project with Africana Tech & Branding Ltd, the client agrees to the terms above.
