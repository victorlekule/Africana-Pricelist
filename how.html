The Process

Six steps from first message to final handover.

Every project — from a simple logo to a full web platform — follows this same path. The depth of each step scales with the size of your project.

  1. 1
    Step 1 · Enquiry ~5 minutes

    Send your enquiry

    Reach out on WhatsApp, by email, or use the quotation builder on this site. Tell us what you need — the more detail you give us, the more accurate our quote will be.

    What to share with us

    • • Type of service you need
    • • A rough budget range
    • • Your preferred deadline
    • • Any examples you love

    What happens next

    We reply within 2 hours (business days) or 24 hours max. If your brief is unclear, we'll ask a few smart questions before quoting.

  2. 2
    Step 2 · Quotation Within 24 hours

    Receive your written quote

    We send you a free, itemised written quotation. Nothing is hidden — you see every line item, what it covers, how long it takes and how much it costs. No pressure, no obligation.

    What your quote includes

    Scope

    What we'll deliver

    Pricing

    Itemised in TZS

    Timeline

    Delivery dates

    Terms

    Deposit & balance

    Good to know: Quotes are valid for 14 days. If anything in your brief changes after we quote, we'll revise it with you before any work begins.

  3. 3
    Step 3 · Confirmation Same day

    Confirm & pay the deposit

    Happy with the quote? Let us know in writing (email, WhatsApp or a signed reply). We issue a VAT invoice, and once we receive your 40% deposit, the project officially kicks off.

    Deposit

    40%

    To kick off the project

    Balance

    60%

    Due on final delivery

    Payment

    M-Pesa · Bank · Cash

    Corporate invoicing available

    Corporate clients: 30-day invoicing is available on request. We issue official VAT receipts for every payment.

  4. 4
    Step 4 · Build 4 – 12 weeks*

    We build & share progress

    Our team gets to work. Depending on the service, we'll share drafts, mockups, prototypes or production proofs at agreed milestones. You'll never wonder what's happening behind the scenes.

    Regular updates

    Progress shared at every meaningful milestone — no ghost mode.

    One point of contact

    The same project lead from start to finish.

    Scope changes handled fairly

    If you want more than agreed, we re-quote — never a surprise bill.

    *Timeline depends on the service. Logo design: 1–2 weeks. Websites: 4–6 weeks. E-commerce platforms: 8–12 weeks.

  5. 5
    Step 5 · Review 2 – 7 days

    Review & refine

    This is your time to look carefully at what we've built. Every project includes a revision round during the build phase — you tell us what to adjust, and we refine it together.

    What "revision" covers

    • ✓ Adjustments to colours, spacing, tone
    • ✓ Copy edits and image swaps
    • ✓ Layout refinements within the agreed scope

    What counts as new scope

    • → Adding new pages or features
    • → Complete redesign after approval
    • → Changing the direction entirely

    Any new scope is quoted separately and approved by you before we proceed.

    Once you're happy, you give us a simple written sign-off (email or WhatsApp message) — that marks the build phase complete.

  6. 6
    Step 6 · Delivery Final stage

    Final delivery & handover

    Once the balance is settled, we hand over everything you've paid for — cleanly, completely, with full ownership transferred to you.

    You receive

    • ✓ All final source files
    • ✓ Editable design files (AI, PSD, Figma)
    • ✓ Website source code (where applicable)
    • ✓ Print-ready PDFs / artwork
    • ✓ VAT invoice & receipt

    Ownership & rights

    Once paid in full, you own the deliverables — logos, brand assets, source files, and content. We retain portfolio display rights unless you request otherwise in writing.

    Bonus: all projects come with a 14-day free adjustment window after delivery for any small fixes. Ongoing maintenance plans start from TZS 150,000/month.

Common questions

Quick answers about our process.

Can I see work before paying the full amount?

Yes. You only pay the 40% deposit upfront, and the remaining 60% is due on delivery — after you've seen and approved the work.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Every project includes a revision round. If something isn't right, we fix it — we'd rather get it perfect than close a ticket.

Can I cancel mid-project?

Yes, you can cancel at any time. Refunds depend on how much work is complete — see our Terms for details.
