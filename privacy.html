1. Introduction

Africana Tech & Branding Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the website pricelist.africana.co.tz (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights you have regarding your data.

This policy is written to comply with the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (PDPA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors from the European Economic Area. By using our Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

In short: We only collect what we need to send you a quote, respond to enquiries, and improve the Site. We never sell your data. You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at any time.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you use our quotation form, contact us by email, WhatsApp, or phone, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Company or organisation name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • Location or city
  • Project details, deadlines, and additional notes you choose to share
  • Payment preference and deposit option (not card or bank credentials)

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our servers and analytics tools may automatically record:

  • IP address (anonymised where possible)
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, time spent, and referring URL
  • Cookies and similar tracking identifiers (see Section 4)

2.3 Information from third parties

If you contact us via WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or email, the respective platform may share basic profile information with us that you have made publicly available.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To prepare and send quotations you have requested.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via our forms, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To deliver services you have engaged us for, including invoicing and receipts.
  • To improve the Site — understanding which pages are useful and which are not.
  • To communicate with you about your project or respond to follow-up questions.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax and business record-keeping under Tanzanian law.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep the Site secure.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites work properly and understand how visitors use them. We use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the Site to function, such as remembering your quotation items during a session.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic (e.g., which pages are popular). These are anonymised where possible.
  • Preference cookies — remember settings you have chosen so you don't have to re-enter them.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the quotation form and other parts of the Site.

5. Sharing Your Information

We share your personal information only with trusted service providers who help us operate the Site and deliver our services. These include:

  • Hosting providers — to keep the Site online and secure.
  • Email and communication tools — to send and receive messages.
  • WhatsApp / Meta platforms — when you choose to contact us via WhatsApp.
  • Analytics providers — with anonymised data only.
  • Payment processors and banks — when you make a payment, only the information required to process the transaction.
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

Each provider is required to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we have specified. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We use industry-standard measures, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all pages on the Site.
  • Restricted access to personal data — only team members who need it can see it.
  • Regular updates to our software and security patches.
  • Secure storage of backups and business records.

While no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, we work hard to protect your data using best practices. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at privacy@pricelist.africana.co.tz.

7. Data Retention

We keep your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, plus any additional period required by law. Specifically:

  • Quotation enquiries: retained for up to 24 months, so we can reference them if you return.
  • Project records and invoices: retained for at least 5 years, as required by Tanzanian tax and business law.
  • Analytics data: retained in anonymised form for up to 26 months.
  • Marketing communications: retained until you unsubscribe.

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

8. Your Rights

Under the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (and the GDPR where it applies), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to deletion — request that we delete your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to object — object to certain processing, such as marketing communications.
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a portable format.
  • Right to complain — lodge a complaint with the Tanzanian Personal Data Protection Commission.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@pricelist.africana.co.tz. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites (for example, our social media profiles or partner sites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11. International Transfers

Africana Tech & Branding Ltd is based in Tanzania. Some of our service providers (such as hosting and analytics platforms) may store data on servers located outside Tanzania. Where this happens, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in line with this policy and applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
