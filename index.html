About Africana

Built in Tanzania. Trusted across Africa.

Africana Tech & Branding Ltd is a full-service creative and technology studio headquartered in Arusha. For over a decade, we've partnered with ambitious businesses, NGOs and enterprises to build brands that stand out and digital products that perform.

Founded in 2015 in Arusha, Africana began as a small design studio with a simple belief: African businesses deserve world-class creative and technical work — without the overseas price tag or the month-long wait times.

Today we're a full-service studio delivering brand identities, high-performance websites, custom software, print & signage and digital marketing for clients across Tanzania and beyond.
